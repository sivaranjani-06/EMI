Loan guide

Plain answers to common EMI questions.

The EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Worked example

₹10,00,000 at 6.5% for 5 years gives r = 0.005417 and n = 60, so the EMI is about ₹19,566 and total interest about ₹1,73,969.

What is an EMI?

A fixed amount you pay every month. It covers part of the principal and part of the interest.

Does a longer tenure save money?

It lowers the monthly EMI but increases the total interest you pay.

Why does interest shrink over time?

Interest is charged on the remaining balance, so each payment reduces more principal than the last.

Do fees affect the EMI?

Processing fees and insurance are not included here. Ask your lender for the final figure.
